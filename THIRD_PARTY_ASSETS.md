# Third-party particle art

The nine PNG frames in `src/main/resources/assets/mallardguard/textures/particle/flying_spark/` and `spark_flash/` come from Particle Interactions 0.4.1 for NeoForge 1.21.1, by Enchanted_Games (https://enchanted.games/). They were moved into Mallard Guard's namespace. `GuardSparkParticle.java` adapts the supplied JAR's flying spark movement, crossed shape, stretching and light progression, with a shorter life and a single emission per parry. Other Particle Interactions features are not bundled.
