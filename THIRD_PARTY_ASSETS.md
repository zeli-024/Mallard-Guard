# Third-party particle art

The nine PNG frames in `src/main/resources/assets/mallardguard/textures/particle/flying_spark/` and `spark_flash/` come from Particle Interactions 0.4.1 for NeoForge 1.21.1, by Enchanted_Games (https://enchanted.games/). They are used as anvil-style impact sparks in Mallard Guard. They were moved into Mallard Guard's namespace; Mallard Guard's particle behavior is implemented separately. Other sprites and code from Particle Interactions are not bundled.
