# 1.54d beta 7

- Player tracer colors default to #FFEBD7, #E9B692 and #FFFFFF. Mob tracer colors default to #77433A, #9A342D and #CFCFCF. Each impact automatically uses its matching tracer palette. Flying Sparks and Spark Debris remain white.
- Mob spawn gear defaults are 40 exact vanilla item assignments: leather/chainmail/iron/golden/diamond armor, and wooden/stone/iron/golden/diamond swords, pickaxes, shovels and hoes. No axes, netherite, elytra, turtle helmets or maces. Bows, crossbows and shields are not part of the default list. Equipment with zero or negative attack modifiers can still be assigned; guarding eligibility remains a separate rule. Existing equipped items are preserved.
- Players and guarding mobs share the existing parry-healing setting. Perfect parries heal the configured hearts; regular parries heal half. A consumed stance heals once, including projectile parries; replayed projectile outcomes and follow-up safety hits do not heal again. Off by default.
- Incidental hits from mobs no longer become experimental pressure-guard targets or receive parry retaliation unless that mob was already the defender's AI target. Perfect projectile returns do not aim at unrelated mob shooters. Retaliation damage does not feed pressure-guard targeting. Intentional fights against an existing mob target remain supported; other mods' native targeting logic is not overwritten.
- Added a shared monitor for particle ticks/history, custom rendering, creation/palette publication, pruning/removal, session cleanup and collision profiling. This addresses the unsafe collection/history access under asynchronous particle ticking without per-frame snapshot allocations or an AsyncParticles dependency. No other mod's settings are changed. Runtime modpack verification remains necessary.
- Chromatic Aberration's default duration is 6 ticks. Mob perfect parry remains 15 ticks, with 2-second stance maximums. Disc and impact Alpha Control remain removed.

- Share and cache player particle colors on join and Apply; send only a palette ID per player burst. Late joiners receive existing palettes. Logout/disconnect and shutdown clear their caches; respawns and dimension changes preserve them. Mob palettes remain server-controlled, and server enforcement overrides shared player colors.
- Add a separate Regular Parry Hitlag duration slider (0–10 frames, default Off), alongside Perfect Parry Hitlag (default 6). Preserve old regular-hitlag behavior when migrating a shared duration.
- Add On/Off controls to the Flying Sparks, Spark Debris, Spark Tracers and Center Impact dividers. Sliders retain their values when an effect is disabled.
- Shield Forward Bias defaults On and sits inside Spark Tracers; its spread still affects all spark families.

Config revision 88; networking protocol 67. Source/archive verification only.
