# Mallard Guard 1.53c-beta-1

Adds Shooting Tracers as a third parry particle effect: fast straight streaks with small white heads, continuous tapered trails, varied speed and fade duration, and no gravity or bouncing. Tracers stop at blocks and fade. Their trails use one strip per head, without trail particle entities or position histories.

Shooting Tracers have Base Intensity, Perfect Intensity, and Damage Scaling sliders, each ranging from 0 to 100%, with defaults 8%, 16%, and 0%. Settings support config persistence, presets, server enforcement, search, and reset. Older reserved slots are reset when migrating to prevent old debris tuning values becoming tracer amounts.

All three effects share the 512-head burst and 1024-head active limits and one atlas batch. Oversized bursts scale effect amounts proportionally. Flying Sparks and the corrected Spark Debris profile retain their motion settings.

Beta status remains because rapid Punchy animation use can intermittently fail to render. Java syntax and source/math checks were performed; compile and visual validation require a local build.
