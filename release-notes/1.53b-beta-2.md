# Mallard Guard 1.53b-beta-2

- Renamed Dot Streaks to Spark Debris.
- Reduced the white head and tail-dot sizes and shortened the dotted history to four samples.
- Spark Debris now has its own launch speed, immediate gravity, lower air drag, and lifetime variation.
- Impacts produce a noticeable low rebound followed by diminishing hops, with separate normal and tangential energy loss.
- Low-energy supported debris settles after at least two impacts while its remaining animation and fading continue.
- Flying Sparks motion and existing particle intensity settings are preserved.
