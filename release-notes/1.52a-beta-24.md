# Mallard Guard 1.52a-beta-24

- Flying sparks use swept bounding-box collision for slow movement and retain ray collision for fast movement.
- Low-energy sparks settle on confirmed ground contact, then continue animating and fading without further physics checks.
- Wall contact alone does not freeze a falling spark, and low speed alone does not stop a spark in midair.
- Particle amounts, weighted spread, appearance, fading, and viewing-distance behavior are unchanged.
