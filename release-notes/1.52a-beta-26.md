# Mallard Guard 1.52a-beta-26

- Added white Dot Streaks: one moving square followed by a short history of separate, fading square dots.
- Dot Streaks use the same weighted spread, movement variation, and collision handling as Flying Sparks.
- Added independent dot streak Base Intensity, Perfect Intensity, and Damage Scaling controls, each ranging from 0–100%.
- Tail dots have no independent physics and use a fixed eight-sample history.
- Both particle effects share one renderer, a 512-head burst budget, and the existing 1,024-active-head budget.
- Existing Flying Sparks settings are preserved when older presets migrate.
