# 1.54c-beta-6

- Adds the original first six-frame center-impact artwork at the same hit origin as Flying Sparks, Spark Debris and Spark Tracers. Later generated variants are not used. The original artwork's colors remain intact; fixed 65% opacity makes it less prominent.
- One stationary camera-facing sprite per hit. No gravity, travel, bounce, collision checks, or physics fragments. Animated fragments belong to the texture.
- Client → World Particles → Center Impact has only Impact Size and Impact Animation Speed. Both default to 100%. Size ranges 0–400%; 0 disables this effect. Speed ranges 25–400%; 100% takes 6 ticks (300 ms), 200% takes 3 ticks, 25% takes 24 ticks. Default sprite width is 0.6 blocks.
- Drawn after all three spark families in their existing atlas batch, with a small camera-facing depth bias at the origin. Normal world depth testing remains active; the effect does not show through walls.
- At most 64 simultaneous impacts, with removal, stale-state pruning, level-change and disconnect cleanup. No extra batch or network message per hit.
- Editable 32 × 192 master sheet and export script are in art-source/. Six 32 × 32 runtime PNGs can also be edited directly or overridden through a resource pack. Original enlarged reference is included. Colors are not configurable in this release.
- New settings participate in client Apply, reset, presets, enforcement, and search. Earlier presets gain defaults without shifting old slots. Config revision and particle preset revision are 75; network protocol is 55. Update both clients and server.
- Beta 5's Parry Healing and Continuous Guard remain off by default, with their settings and behavior preserved.
