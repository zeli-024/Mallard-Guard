# 1.54c-beta-5

## Parry Healing

Under Parrying → Healing. Disabled by default. The single amount slider defaults to 1 heart, ranges from 0 to 5 hearts, and advances in whole hearts. A regular parry restores half the selected value; a perfect parry restores the full value. Thus the default amount restores half a heart on a regular parry and one heart on a perfect parry.

Healing happens on the server once per successful attempt. Blocks, follow-up safety hits, and retaliation victims never award healing. Health is capped at the player's maximum. An amount of 0 also disables healing.

## Continuous Guard

Under Parrying → Timing. Disabled by default. Requires blocking to be enabled. Hold the existing guard key to maintain the stance; press attack while guarding to attempt another parry. Recharge still applies, including while blocking. Holding attack does not repeatedly reopen the window. Attack presses during an open window are consumed without extending it.

Successful weapon parries return to blocking while guard remains held. Shields keep their existing use action. Reopening a parry window preserves accumulated block counts. Pending or completed guard breaks prevent reopening; release guard before raising it again. Guarded attack input suppresses swinging, attacks, and mining. Server entity-attack handling also cancels guarded attacks.
