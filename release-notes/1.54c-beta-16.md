# 1.54c-beta-16

- Replace the old static cross / independently configured ring with the approved six-frame 64px impact. Pixel contours use nearest-neighbor export; dissipation leaves sparse unequal arcs.
- Center Impact now has exactly five controls: Size, Spin Speed, Opacity, Duration and Alpha Control. Its tint follows the three Spark Tracer colors; a matching per-frame mask preserves white highlights and transparent areas. Starting angle and left/right spin direction are randomized at spawn.
- Add Parry Disc: the outer rim fades toward a configurable center, starts slightly taller than the player, expands and slows down while fading. Its world plane keeps the parrying player's captured facing, including pitch, for all nearby viewers.
- Disc controls: Expansion Speed, Center Opacity, Opacity, Color, Lifetime and Expansion Duration. Expansion duration is capped by lifetime.
- Remove the buff-expiry sound when the parry window transitions to blocking.
- Retire unused impact settings and old art exports; retain serialized preset positions and accept beta-15 presets.
- Packet revision is 63 because the sparks payload now includes captured facing. Multiplayer clients and server must use the same release.
- Source-only delivery: no Gradle build, javac compilation or game launch was run here. Source, config metadata, assets, preset lengths and packet wiring were reviewed; compilation and in-game behavior remain for the user's build/test.
