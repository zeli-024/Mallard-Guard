# Mallard Guard 1.53b-beta-1

- Introduced the 1.53b beta series, carrying forward the current Flying Sparks and Dot Streaks work.
- Flying Sparks include varied motion, animation, lifetime and fading, with occasional electric appearances.
- Dot Streaks use a moving white square and a bounded history of fading square dots.
- Each effect has independent Base Intensity, Perfect Intensity and Damage Scaling controls.
- Both effects share collision handling, rendering and particle budgets.
- Includes the fixes for the fast-spark collision context and dot texture lookup crashes.
- Punchy integration remains under beta testing.
