# Mallard Guard 1.53c-beta-2

Corrects Shooting Tracers to straight dotted trails and adds temporary tuning controls: Size, Speed, Velocity Loss, Lifetime, Fade Duration, Trail Length, Dot Spacing, Dot Size, Size Variation, Motion Variation, Fade Variation, Spread Bias, and Trail Fade. The three intensity sliders remain. Percent controls range from 0 to 100%; time uses ticks; spacing and length display blocks and are stored in hundredths of a block.

Dot Spacing defaults to 0.15 blocks and Trail Length to 0.75 blocks. Dots are fixed at distance intervals along the flight path, independent of tick or frame rate and launch speed. Each tracer draws at most 16 trail dots; small spacing may shorten the effective trail rather than change the requested spacing. No dot particle entities, collision checks, or history arrays are added. Tracers remain straight, stop on block contact, and fade without gravity or bounce.

Tuning settings support client configs, server presets and enforcement, search, resets, and keyboard adjustment. Older reserved tuning slots receive defaults during migration while existing intensity values are preserved. Flying Sparks and the corrected Spark Debris motion profile retain their settings.

Beta status remains because rapid Punchy animation use can intermittently fail to render. Java syntax and source/math checks were performed; compile and visual validation require a local build.
