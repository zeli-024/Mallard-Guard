**Mallard Guard** adds parrying and blocking to Minecraft. Hold right-click to block, or time it with an incoming hit to parry. A tighter window gives you a perfect parry.

You can also parry projectiles and fall damage. Regular projectile parries deflect shots; perfect parries send them back toward whoever fired them. A shield icon near the crosshair shows when you’re guarding, and hits have their own sounds and effects.

Most weapons and tools can guard. You can adjust the timing, durability cost, sounds, effects, and which items are allowed.
